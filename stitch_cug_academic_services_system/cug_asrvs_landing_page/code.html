Frequently Asked Questions

Registry Guidance & Policies

How long does it take for transcripts to be processed? expand_more

Standard processing timeframe is 3 to 5 business days for alumni graduating after 2012. For archived historical records prior to 2012, verification takes 5 to 7 business days as physical archives at the Fiapre registry are reconciled. Expedited fast-track options are available during checkout.

Do I need my student portal password to request documents? expand_more

No. The ASRVS portal was purposely created as an open-access authenticated system. You only need your Index Number or Registration Number, programme title, and personal contact telephone/email. The registry authenticates your records internally.

Can I have my transcript sent directly to WES or an international embassy? expand_more

Yes. When selecting dispatch, choose "Secure Institutional Email" or "DHL International". You will be prompted to enter your World Education Services (WES) reference number, CAS reference, or the recipient university's designated electronic admissions mailbox.

What mobile payment methods are accepted? expand_more

We accept all Ghana mobile money networks (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AT Money) as well as locally issued and international Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards through the Bank of Ghana approved payment gateway.
