Student Inquiries & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential procedures regarding transcript issuances, processing durations, and official authentication protocols.

help_outline How soon is my transcript processed?

Standard processing ranges between 24 and 72 working hours after automated payment validation. Fast-track requests are handled sequentially by the Examinations & Records Unit.

help_outline Can my transcript be sent directly to WES?

Yes. When filling the destination details, indicate your WES Reference Number and choose 'Institutional Electronic Transmittal'. We dispatch securely using the official registry channel.

help_outline What if I graduated prior to 2012?

Alumni from affiliate periods (e.g., when affiliated with University of Ghana or UCC) can request here. Physical manual ledger audits take an extra 48 hours for legacy verification.

help_outline What payment channels are accepted?

The portal connects directly with the GhIPSS national switch. You can pay via MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, or Visa/Mastercard. Receipts are generated immediately.
